## When a design round asks the person

Your question reaches the person as a blocker the driver parks on the workstream. Their answer is
read by the next decision round, which judges the design again with it; nothing they answer
approves the design, and the design is built only once a round proceeds. So ask for what only the
person holds — a scope, a priority, a trade-off the records do not settle — never for permission
to build.

Ask one question, in the person's terms rather than the record's field names, naming what each
answer would change in the design. If the round needs a repair before the question means anything,
recommend the repair and ask nothing yet.
