## When you halt on a blocker

Your `:needs` IS the question — the card leads with it — so it is one sentence ending in `?`,
at most 240 characters. Your `:summary` is the context under it: what you were doing and why you
cannot go on. `:recommendation` is the answer you would give, only when you genuinely hold a view on which answer is right — never as filler; `:after` says what happens once the
person answers — what you do next, what waits. The ledger refuses a blocker without `:after` or
with a `:needs` that is not one question. A choice goes in `:options`, each with `:summary`
(what taking it means), `:consequence` (what it costs) and `:recommended?` on the one you would pick.
