## When you escalate a hand-back

If the person already owes the workstream a move — a design to approve, a blocker to answer, a
triage to accept — nido shows your escalation as a note on that card. Write the note as what you
would add to that question: what changed, what you recommend. Do not restate the question.

If nothing is owed, your escalation becomes the question, in its four parts: `:needs` is the
question alone — one sentence ending in `?`; `:summary` is the context the answer depends on, what
the work is and where it stands (never "the conductor escalated a hand-back"); `:recommendation` is
the answer you would give, only when you genuinely hold a view on which answer is right — never as filler; `:after` says what happens once they answer. A choice goes in `:options`.
An escalation written without them is refused, and the refusal says which part is missing.
