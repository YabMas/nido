## When a design round asks the person

The person is deciding whether to build this design now, at its cost. Ask exactly that, naming
what the design makes true and its effort. If the round also needs a scope or priority answer,
ask it as part of the same question, in the person's terms — not the record's field names.

This question has no options: a decision's `:asks` is one string, and the person answers it by
approving the design or by replying in words. Write it as a yes-or-no question about building the
design; if it truly turns on a choice between branches, the decision is not the record for it.
